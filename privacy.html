Support Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use the mobile game Number Merge (the “Game”).

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or other personal information to play the Game.

We may collect the following limited information automatically:

  • Device information: Device model, operating system version, language, and country
  • Advertising identifiers: IDFA (on iOS) for showing personalized or non-personalized ads
  • Usage data: Approximate play time, session length, and basic game events (to improve the game and show relevant ads)
  • Analytics data: Crash reports and performance data (if analytics tools are used)

2. How We Use Information

We use the information to:

  • Provide and improve the Game
  • Show advertisements (including rewarded video ads)
  • Analyze how the Game is used
  • Fix bugs and technical issues

3. Advertising

The Game displays advertisements, including rewarded ads (for example, to get extra power-ups or to revive after a game over).

These ads are served by third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob or similar networks). These partners may collect and use information about you in accordance with their own privacy policies, such as Google’s Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”

4. Third-Party Services

We may use the following types of third-party services:

  • Advertising networks (AdMob, etc.)
  • Analytics tools (if enabled)

These services have their own privacy policies and may collect data independently.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us.

6. Data Retention

We retain collected data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.
